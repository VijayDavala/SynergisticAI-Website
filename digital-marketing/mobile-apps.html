Challenges We Solve

Why Mobile Apps Struggle to Grow

The app stores are more competitive than ever. With 5 million apps live, standing out requires a data-driven user acquisition engine — not just a great product.

📉

High CPI, Low Quality Users

Spending $5–$8 per install but those users don't activate, retain, or monetize. Without audience targeting and creative optimization, you pay for installs that never convert to revenue.

🔍

Invisible on App Stores

With millions of apps competing for visibility, organic discovery is nearly impossible without ASO. Poor keyword targeting and weak store creative means your app doesn't get found.

📱

ATT & Privacy Changes

iOS App Tracking Transparency destroyed IDFA-based targeting. Without SKAdNetwork optimization and modeled attribution, you can't accurately measure which campaigns drive quality users.

🔄

Poor D7/D30 Retention

Acquiring users is worthless if they churn in the first week. Without push notification strategies, in-app onboarding optimization, and re-engagement campaigns, DAU stays flat.

💰

Low In-App Monetization

Free installs don't pay the bills. Without subscription upsell flows, IAP trigger optimization, and LTV-based bidding, you're undermonetizing your user base significantly.

🎨

Creative Fatigue at Scale

Video creatives burn out fast in mobile UA. Without a systematic creative testing pipeline producing 10–20 new variations per week, your best-performing ads die and CPI spikes.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What app stage do you typically work with?

We work with apps post-launch that have proven retention (D7 ≥ 20%) and are ready to scale UA spend. For pre-launch apps, we offer ASO and creative strategy. For growth-stage apps, we scale from $10K to $500K+/month in ad spend.

How do you handle iOS ATT and privacy measurement?

We configure SKAN campaigns with proper conversion value schemas, implement probabilistic modeling, and use MMP (AppsFlyer/Adjust) alongside platform-reported metrics to triangulate true performance despite signal loss.

Do you work with both iOS and Android apps?

Yes. We run simultaneous iOS and Android campaigns with platform-specific strategies — Apple Search Ads + Meta for iOS, Google UAC + Meta for Android — with cross-platform LTV analysis to allocate budgets optimally.

What attribution tool do you recommend?

We work with AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch, and Singular. For most apps, we recommend AppsFlyer for its SKAN support and partner network breadth. We handle full MMP setup and configuration as part of onboarding.
