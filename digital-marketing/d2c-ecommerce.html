Challenges We Solve

Why D2C Brands Struggle to Scale

Scaling a DTC brand profitably is harder than ever. Rising ad costs, iOS privacy changes, and fierce competition mean you need smarter strategies — not just bigger budgets.

📉

High CAC, Low ROAS

Ad costs keep rising but your ROAS is stuck below 2×. Without proper creative testing, audience segmentation, and funnel optimization, you're burning cash without scaling.

📱

iOS Tracking Chaos

iOS 14+ broke Meta pixel attribution for most brands. Without server-side tracking, Conversion API setup, and modeled attribution, you're flying blind on true ad performance.

🛒

Cart Abandonment Losses

68% of shoppers abandon carts. Without automated abandonment sequences via email and SMS, you're losing revenue that's already halfway through your funnel.

🔄

Low Repeat Purchase Rate

One-time buyers are expensive. Without retention marketing, loyalty programs, and post-purchase flows, your LTV stays low and ad costs eat your margin.

🎨

Creative Fatigue

Running the same ads for more than 2–3 weeks tanks performance. Without a systematic creative testing machine, your best ads die and ROAS collapses.

🌐

Poor Website Conversion

Great traffic, bad conversion. A product page that doesn't build trust, lacks social proof, or has slow load times is costing you 60–70% of potential revenue.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What minimum monthly revenue should our store have before working with you?

We work best with D2C brands doing $30K+/month in revenue who are ready to scale. For brands below this threshold, we recommend starting with our email & CRO services before adding significant paid spend.

How do you handle iOS attribution challenges?

We implement Meta Conversion API (server-side), configure modeled attribution, and use MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio) alongside blended ROAS for a complete picture of true ad performance.

What ad spend do you recommend for D2C scaling?

For meaningful results, we recommend a minimum of $5,000/month in ad spend. Brands looking to scale from 6 to 7 figures typically invest $15,000–$50,000/month in paid channels.

Do you produce creative assets or do we need to provide them?

We provide creative strategy, briefs, and direction. We work with your existing assets and guide you on UGC collection. For full creative production, we can connect you with our partner network of DTC-specialized creators.
