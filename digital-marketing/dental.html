Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I see new patient appointments?

Most dental clients receive their first new patient inquiries within 5–7 days of campaign launch. With Google Ads, the results are near-immediate. SEO results compound over 60–90 days.

Can you target specific procedures like implants or Invisalign?

Absolutely. We build dedicated campaigns for each high-value procedure with custom landing pages, treatment-specific messaging, and before/after creative that converts procedure-seekers into booked consultations.

Do you work with multi-location dental groups?

Yes. We manage campaigns for single practices, group practices, and DSOs with multiple locations. Each location gets a geo-targeted campaign and optimized Google Business Profile.

What ad spend do you recommend for dental marketing?

We recommend a minimum of $1,500–$2,500/month in ad spend for effective dental campaigns. For cosmetic dentistry and implant-focused practices, $3,000–$5,000/month accelerates results significantly.
