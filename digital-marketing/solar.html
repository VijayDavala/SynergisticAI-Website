Frequently Asked Questions

Are the solar leads you generate truly exclusive?

Yes. Every lead is generated exclusively for your company through campaigns we build and manage solely for you. We never resell leads or work with competing solar companies in the same territory.

How do you qualify solar leads before sending them to us?

Our AI-powered qualification flow screens for homeownership status, roof type and age, average monthly utility bill ($100+), and general credit eligibility. Only leads meeting your defined criteria reach your sales team.

What ad spend do you recommend for solar?

We typically recommend $2,500–$5,000/month in ad spend for residential solar. Commercial solar campaigns typically start at $4,000/month. Our management fees are separate and tiered by campaign volume.

Can you target specific states or utility territories?

Absolutely. We can geo-target by state, county, city, zip code, or custom radius. We can also layer in utility provider data and incentive eligibility filters where available.
