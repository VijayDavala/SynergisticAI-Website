Challenges We Solve

Why Healthcare Providers Struggle Online

Healthcare marketing has unique compliance requirements, trust dynamics, and patient journey complexities that generic agencies routinely get wrong.

🔒

HIPAA Compliance Risks

Most general agencies run healthcare ads that inadvertently violate HIPAA by using health-condition targeting or retargeting pixels that capture protected health information.

🔍

Poor Local Search Visibility

Patients search for specialists near them. Without strong local SEO and Google Business optimization, your practice is invisible when it matters most.

📅

No-Show & Cancellation Rates

High no-show rates drain revenue. Without automated confirmation and reminder sequences, practices lose 15–25% of booked appointments.

⭐

Reputation Management

Patients read reviews before choosing any provider. A weak review profile or unanswered negative reviews send patients to competing practices.

📱

Telehealth Competition

Telehealth platforms are aggressively marketing to your local patients. Without a strong digital presence, you lose patients who'd prefer a trusted local provider.

💡

Complex Patient Education

Healthcare decisions are rarely impulsive. Patients research extensively before booking. Without educational content, they choose the provider who educated them best.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure our campaigns are HIPAA compliant?

We follow strict HIPAA guidelines: no health-condition based audience targeting, compliant pixel configurations, BAA agreements where required, and regular compliance audits of all campaigns and communications.

Can you market directly to patients, or only B2B referral marketing?

Both. We run direct-to-patient campaigns (Google, Meta, SEO) and B2B referral marketing targeting physicians, case managers, and health systems depending on your practice model.

Do you work with multi-location health systems?

Yes. We manage enterprise healthcare marketing for multi-location practices and health systems, with location-specific campaigns, unified brand strategy, and centralized reporting.

What results can we expect in the first 60 days?

Most clients see a 40–80% increase in new patient inquiries within 60 days from paid campaigns. SEO results build over 90–180 days but provide the most cost-effective long-term patient growth.
