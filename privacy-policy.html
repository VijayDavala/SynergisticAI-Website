Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  |  Effective: June 2026

Synergistic AI Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. We may collect the following information when you use our website or submit enquiries:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Send you relevant communications about our AI automation and digital marketing services
  • Improve and optimise our website and user experience
  • Measure advertising effectiveness and reach prospective clients
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, our legal bases for processing personal data are:

  • Legitimate interests — to respond to business enquiries and provide our services
  • Consent — for marketing communications and advertising cookies
  • Contractual necessity — to deliver services you have engaged us for

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with the following trusted third-party services:

  • Supabase — our database provider (lead form submissions are stored securely)
  • Google Analytics — anonymous website usage analytics
  • Meta (Facebook) — Meta Pixel for advertising performance measurement
  • LinkedIn — LinkedIn Insight Tag for B2B advertising analytics

All third-party providers are required to handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and enquiry data for up to 24 months, after which it is securely deleted unless we have an ongoing business relationship with you or are required by law to retain it longer.

6. Cookies

Our website uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling advertising cookies will not affect your ability to use our website.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to object — object to processing of your data for marketing purposes
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at digitalsynergistic@gmail.com.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption, secure API connections (TLS 1.2+), and access controls on our database. While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure.

Synergistic AI Solutions is based in Hyderabad, India, and serves clients globally. If you are located in the EEA or UK, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside those regions. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business professionals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.
